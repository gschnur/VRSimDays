Objective(s)

Conduct a Time Atack Exhibition for bragging rights. Each driver will get an equal amount of acclimation time. Said time is for both learning the track and the car on that track. After acclimation while still fresh, a driver will go straight into hot laps. All drivers will have the same number of hot laps, only the best time will be counted.
